KEY CONCEPTS

- Equation: A statement of equality between two expressions, like x + 5 = 12.
- Variable: A letter like x, y, or z that stands for an unknown number.
- LHS and RHS: The left side of the equal sign is LHS, and the right side is RHS.
- Balanced Equation: Both sides of a correct equation always have the same value.


RULES FOR SOLVING

- Keep the equation balanced by doing the exact same math operation on both sides.
- Addition rule: If you add a number to one side, add it to the other side too.
- Subtraction rule: Subtract the same number from both sides to keep it equal.
- Multiplication rule: Multiply both sides by the same non-zero number.
- Division rule: Divide both sides by the same non-zero number.


TRANSPOSITION METHOD

- Moving a number or term from one side of the equal sign to the other side is called transposition.
- Plus becomes minus: +a on one side becomes -a on the other side.
- Minus becomes plus: -a on one side becomes +a on the other side.
- Multiply becomes divide: A multiply factor becomes a division denominator.
- Divide becomes multiply: A division denominator becomes a multiply factor.


EXAMPLE STEP-BY-STEP

Solve the equation: 2x - 3 = 7

Step 1: Transpose -3 to the right side. It becomes +3.

New form: 2x = 7 + 3

Step 2: Simplify the right side.

New form: 2x = 10

Step 3: Transpose 2 (which multiplies x) to divide the right side.

New form: x = 10 / 2

Final Answer: x = 5
