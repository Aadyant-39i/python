PHYSICS

- Motion in a straight line uses equations like v = u + at.
- Velocity is the rate of change of displacement.
- Acceleration is the rate of change of velocity.
- Newton's first law defines inertia and reference frames.
- Work done is the dot product of force and displacement vectors.
- Kinetic energy equals one-half mass times velocity squared (1/2 mv²).


CHEMISTRY

- Mole concept states one mole contains 6.022 × 10²³ particles.
- Atomic radius decreases across a period from left to right.
- Ionization energy increases across a period.
- Covalent bonds form through the sharing of electron pairs.
- Ionic bonds form through the complete transfer of electrons.
- Enthalpy change (ΔH) measures heat absorbed or released at constant pressure.


BIOLOGY

- Cell membrane uses a fluid mosaic model.
- Mitochondria are the main site for ATP production.
- Photosynthesis light reactions happen in the thylakoid membrane.
- Calvin cycle fixes carbon dioxide in the stroma.
- Enzymes lower the activation energy of biochemical reactions.
- Mitosis produces two identical diploid daughter cells.
